Built for the moment it happens

Registro Plus brings together what matters in a single record: what happened, the evidence (photo, audio, video or document), the follow-up deadline and the backup on your device.

It's not an everyday notepad. It's a record with evidence, so you can look it up later with clarity.

What you do in the app

  • Create a record with text, classification and deadline
  • Attach photos, videos, audio and documents as evidence
  • Follow the same case over time
  • Share summaries or a report when you need to
  • Back up and restore on your device (.rplus file)
  • Protect it with a PIN; trash for recovery
  • Trial and plans through Google Play

Your data, at your pace

The local backup (.rplus) keeps records and attachments so you can restore them on your phone. Core use does not depend on a cloud account.
