Privacy Policy

Registro+ · Package: br.com.registromais.app

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Registro+ is an app for recording events, attachments, deadlines and history in your day-to-day life. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the app on your device.

1. Who we are

Registro+ is operated by its responsible developer. If you have questions about privacy or about this policy, please contact: registromaisplus@gmail.com.

2. Offline-first model

Registro+ is designed to work mainly on your device. We do not maintain our own server to store your records, attachments or the content of your entries. Data is saved locally on the device, associated with the account you create or restore in the app.

3. Data the app processes

As you use Registro+, the app may store locally:

  • Account data: name, email and access credentials (password protected with encryption on the device).
  • Records: texts, classifications, deadlines, checklists, progress updates and metadata that you enter.
  • Attachments: images, documents, audio and video files that you add or receive through sharing.
  • Usage preferences, security settings (for example, a PIN) and feedback sent from your profile.
  • Minimal technical data needed for the app to work on this device (for example, a local session).

4. Separation by user

Each account created on the device uses its own local database. This prevents records, feedback and preferences from being mixed between different users on the same device.

5. Sharing and export

When you share a summary, dossier, attachments or a backup file (.rplus), the app sends only the content you choose to the destination app or service (for example, WhatsApp, Gmail, Google Drive or a file manager). We do not control how those services handle the data after you send it — please check their privacy policies.

6. Backup

We recommend exporting a backup regularly, especially before changing phones, uninstalling the app or deleting your account. The backup file is your responsibility when saved to cloud storage or external storage.

7. Device permissions

The app may request permissions for features you use, such as:

  • Camera and gallery — to attach photos and videos.
  • Microphone — to record audio or videos with sound.
  • Notifications — for reminders you set up.
  • Storage / files — to import, export and restore backups.
  • Biometrics or screen lock — optional, to protect access to the app.

You can deny permissions; some features may no longer be available.

8. Security

We use technical measures on the device to protect credentials and sensitive data (for example, the system's secure vault and password encryption). No method is 100% infallible; keep your device up to date and do not share your password or recovery code.

9. Retention and account deletion

Data stays on the device while the app is installed and the account exists.

How to delete your account and data in the Registro+ app:

  1. Open the app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password.
  4. The account and the local data associated with it will be removed from this device.

You can also delete records individually, empty the trash and export a backup before deleting your account. When you uninstall the app, local data tends to be removed by the operating system.

To request by email: registromaisplus@gmail.com (subject: Registro+ account deletion).

9.1. Delete data without deleting the account

You can delete part of your data without closing your account:

  1. Open the app and sign in to your account.
  2. For a record: open the record → delete it (it goes to the trash).
  3. To remove it for good: go to Trash → empty it or delete items permanently.
  4. For attachments: remove the attachment within the record or delete the entire record.

What is deleted: the records, attachments and items removed from the trash on this device. What remains: the account (email, password) and the records you did not delete.

Questions or a request by email: registromaisplus@gmail.com (subject: Registro+ data deletion).

10. Children and adolescents

Registro+ is a general-purpose recording tool. If you use the app to document information about minors, it is your responsibility to ensure an adequate legal basis and to avoid including unnecessary or excessive data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The date of the last update will be shown at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acknowledgment of the version in force.

12. Your rights (LGPD, GDPR and CCPA/CPRA)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018), you may request information about the processing of personal data that you provide to us through the contact above. Where the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to you, you may also, in general terms, have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA), applies to you, you may also, in general terms, have rights to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing — we do not sell personal information, and we do not operate a server that stores your records. Because your records stay on your device, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app (access, correction, deletion and export via backup). For any information you provide to us yourself, for example by email, you can contact us at the address above.
